# Low-Carb Sauces and Vinaigrettes

## 15 homemade recipes to bring more flavour to your meals

A homemade sauce can change the texture and flavour of a meal without calling for complicated techniques. This booklet offers citrusy, creamy, herby, spicy and bold options — all made with simple ingredients and no added sugar.

These recipes go well with salads, roasted vegetables, eggs, meat, poultry, fish or vegetarian dishes. Adjust the salt, acidity and consistency a little at a time: ingredients such as lemon, mustard, cheese and pickled or preserved ingredients can vary in intensity from brand to brand.

### Before you begin

- Choose plain, unsweetened yoghurt, mustard with no added sugar and unsweetened coconut milk. Check the labels on mayonnaise, tamari and sun-dried tomatoes.
- Wash and dry fresh herbs thoroughly. Use clean utensils and containers.
- As these sauces contain fresh ingredients, keep leftovers in a clean, sealed container in the refrigerator at **4 °C or below** and use within **3 days**. Avocado sauce is best used within 2 days.
- Sauces made by mixing garlic or fresh herbs with oil must also be kept refrigerated; do not leave them at room temperature.
- The amount of water listed can be adjusted: add it a little at a time until the desired consistency is reached.

## 1. Lemon and mustard vinaigrette

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

### Ingredients
- 60 ml olive oil
- 30 ml lemon or lime juice
- 10 g Dijon mustard with no added sugar
- 15 ml water
- 1 g finely grated lemon zest, optional
- 1 g salt
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Put the lemon juice, mustard, water, salt and pepper in a bowl.
2. Whisk with a fork or small whisk.
3. Slowly drizzle in the olive oil, whisking until the vinaigrette is slightly thickened and smooth. Stir in the zest, if using.

**Pairs with:** slightly bitter leaves, rocket, cucumber, grilled chicken and boiled eggs.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Shake before serving.

## 2. Avocado, coriander and lime cream

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

### Ingredients
- 120 g ripe avocado flesh
- 70 g plain full-fat yoghurt, unsweetened
- 25 ml lime juice
- 8 g coriander leaves
- 10 ml olive oil
- 2 g garlic, green germ removed, optional
- 30–60 ml cold water
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Put the avocado, yoghurt, lime juice, coriander, olive oil, garlic and salt in a blender or food processor.
2. Blend until creamy. Add the water a little at a time, blending between additions, until it reaches the desired consistency.
3. Taste and adjust the salt or lime. For a smoother sauce, blend for a few more seconds.

**Pairs with:** salads with chicken, prawns, cucumber, cabbage or fresh leaves.
**Storage:** Keep tightly covered and refrigerated for up to 2 days. The lime helps slow browning, but the colour may change naturally.

## 3. Tahini, garlic and lemon sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 45 g unsweetened tahini
- 25 ml lemon juice
- 60–80 ml cold water
- 10 ml olive oil
- 2 g finely chopped or grated garlic
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the tahini, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper. The mixture may thicken at first; this is expected.
2. Add the cold water a little at a time, stirring continuously until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
3. Taste and adjust the lemon or salt. For a thinner sauce, add a little more water.

**Pairs with:** roasted cauliflower, broccoli, cucumber, aubergine, chicken or lamb.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Stir before serving.

## 4. Yoghurt, dill and chive sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 150 g plain full-fat Greek yoghurt, unsweetened
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 10 ml olive oil
- 5 g chopped fresh dill
- 8 g chopped fresh chives
- 1 g grated garlic, optional
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Put the yoghurt in a bowl and mix in the lemon juice and olive oil.
2. Stir in the dill, chives, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. Leave to stand for 5 minutes so the flavours can combine. For a thinner sauce, stir in 10–15 ml water.

**Pairs with:** cucumber, leafy greens, eggs, salmon, chicken and grilled vegetables.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days.

## 5. Caesar-style sauce with no added sugar

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 60 g mayonnaise with no added sugar
- 40 g plain full-fat yoghurt, unsweetened
- 20 g finely grated Parmesan
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 5 g Dijon mustard with no added sugar
- 1 anchovy fillet, drained, optional
- 1 g grated garlic
- 10–20 ml water, if needed
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the mayonnaise, yoghurt, Parmesan, lemon juice and mustard.
2. If using anchovy, mash it with a fork and stir it into the sauce. Add the garlic and pepper.
3. Add water a little at a time, if needed, until the sauce is creamy enough to spread or to toss with salad leaves.

**Pairs with:** romaine lettuce, grilled chicken, eggs and broccoli. For a fish-free version, leave out the anchovy and add 5 g chopped capers.
**Storage:** Use commercially prepared mayonnaise and keep the sauce refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. This recipe contains no raw egg.

## 6. Basil and walnut pesto

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

### Ingredients
- 30 g fresh basil leaves, washed and thoroughly dried
- 25 g shelled walnuts
- 20 g grated Parmesan
- 50 ml olive oil
- 5 ml lemon juice
- 2 g garlic
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste
- 10–20 ml water, if needed

### Method
1. Put the basil, walnuts, Parmesan, garlic, salt and pepper in a food processor.
2. Pulse a few times. With the processor running on low, add the olive oil gradually.
3. Add the lemon juice and blend only until combined. For a thinner texture, add water a little at a time.

**Pairs with:** grilled courgette, a small amount of tomato, chicken, eggs or roasted vegetables.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a clean, sealed container for up to 3 days. Do not leave at room temperature.

## 7. Herby chimichurri

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 10 minutes, plus 10 minutes resting time

### Ingredients
- 25 g finely chopped fresh parsley
- 2 g dried oregano
- 5 g finely chopped garlic
- 25 ml red wine vinegar
- 60 ml olive oil
- 15 ml water
- 0.5 g chilli flakes, optional
- 1.5 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Put the parsley, oregano, garlic, salt and chilli flakes in a bowl.
2. Add the vinegar and water. Mix and leave to stand for 10 minutes.
3. Add the olive oil and black pepper. Stir and taste before serving.

**Pairs with:** beef, chicken, mushrooms, cauliflower and strongly flavoured leaves.
**Storage:** As it contains garlic and fresh herbs, keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days; do not store at room temperature.

## 8. Sesame, ginger and tamari sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 20 ml tamari with no added sugar
- 20 ml rice vinegar with no added sugar
- 10 ml toasted sesame oil
- 20 ml mild olive oil
- 25 ml water
- 5 g freshly grated ginger
- 10 g unsweetened tahini
- 5 g sesame seeds
- 1 g grated garlic, optional

### Method
1. Mix the tamari, rice vinegar, sesame oil, olive oil and water.
2. Stir in the ginger, tahini and garlic, if using, until the sauce is smooth.
3. Finish with the sesame seeds. If the sauce is too thick, add water 5 ml at a time.

**Pairs with:** cabbage, cucumber, leafy greens, chicken, tofu and salmon.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Shake or stir before using.

## 9. Roasted red pepper and almond sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 10 minutes, plus roasting time

### Ingredients
- 120 g red pepper
- 20 g unsalted almonds
- 20 ml olive oil
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 1 g smoked paprika
- 2 g garlic
- 20–30 ml water
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Put the whole pepper on a baking tray and roast for about 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until the skin is charred in several places.
2. Transfer the pepper to a bowl, cover for 10 minutes and, once it is cool enough to handle, remove the skin and seeds.
3. Blend the pepper flesh with the almonds, olive oil, lemon juice, paprika, garlic and salt. Add water a little at a time until creamy.

**Pairs with:** leafy greens, chicken, eggs, roasted courgette and cauliflower.
**Storage:** Refrigerate in a clean, sealed container for up to 3 days.

## 10. Caper and parsley vinaigrette

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 45 ml olive oil
- 20 ml lemon juice
- 10 ml white wine vinegar
- 15 g drained capers
- 8 g chopped fresh parsley
- 5 g Dijon mustard with no added sugar
- Black pepper, to taste
- Salt, only if needed

### Method
1. Chop the capers. If they are very salty, rinse them briefly with water and pat dry.
2. Mix the lemon juice, vinegar and mustard. Add the olive oil gradually, stirring to emulsify.
3. Stir in the capers, parsley and pepper. Taste before adding salt.

**Pairs with:** grilled fish, eggs, leafy greens, cucumber and cooked, cooled vegetables.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Stir before serving.

## 11. Gorgonzola and yoghurt sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 6 minutes

### Ingredients
- 100 g plain full-fat Greek yoghurt, unsweetened
- 35 g Gorgonzola
- 10 ml lemon juice
- 15–30 ml water
- Black pepper, to taste
- 3 g chopped chives, optional

### Method
1. Mash the Gorgonzola with a fork.
2. Add the yoghurt and lemon juice. Mix until the sauce has small pieces of cheese, or blend briefly for a smoother texture.
3. Add water a little at a time until it reaches the desired consistency. Finish with pepper and chives.

**Pairs with:** slightly bitter leaves, cucumber, broccoli, beef and walnuts.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days.

## 12. Curry, coconut and lime sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 100 ml full-fat coconut milk, unsweetened
- 50 g plain full-fat yoghurt, unsweetened
- 15 ml lime juice
- 2 g curry powder, with no added sugar or starch
- 2 g freshly grated ginger
- 0.5 g salt
- 10–20 ml water, if needed

### Method
1. Mix the coconut milk, yoghurt and lime juice until smooth.
2. Add the curry powder, ginger and salt. Stir well.
3. For a thinner consistency, add water a little at a time. Taste and adjust the lime or curry.

**Pairs with:** chicken, prawns, cauliflower, cabbage and mild-flavoured leaves.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days. Stir before serving, as the coconut milk may separate.

## 13. Sun-dried tomato and basil sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 35 g sun-dried tomatoes preserved in oil, drained and with no added sugar
- 20 ml olive oil
- 15 ml red wine vinegar
- 30 ml water
- 5 g fresh basil leaves
- 2 g garlic
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Put the sun-dried tomatoes, olive oil, vinegar, water, basil, garlic and pepper in a blender or food processor.
2. Blend until thick. For a smoother texture, blend for a few more seconds; for a rustic texture, pulse briefly.
3. If needed, add water 5 ml at a time until it reaches the desired consistency.

**Pairs with:** leafy greens, aubergine, courgette, chicken and fresh cheese.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days.

## 14. Spicy lemon and paprika sauce

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 120 g plain full-fat yoghurt, unsweetened
- 20 g mayonnaise with no added sugar
- 20 ml lemon juice
- 10 g fresh jalapeño, deseeded, or to taste
- 5 g chopped coriander
- 1 g smoked paprika
- 1 g grated garlic
- 1 g salt
- 10–20 ml water, if needed

### Method
1. Chop the jalapeño. Wear gloves if your skin is sensitive, and wash your hands and utensils thoroughly after handling it.
2. Mix the yoghurt, mayonnaise, lemon juice, paprika, garlic and salt.
3. Stir in the jalapeño and coriander. Add water a little at a time for a thinner sauce.

**Pairs with:** chicken, eggs, cabbage, cucumber and grilled vegetables.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days.

## 15. Black olive and oregano cream

**Makes:** about 4 servings
**Preparation time:** 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 50 g pitted black olives
- 60 g plain full-fat Greek yoghurt, unsweetened
- 10 ml olive oil
- 10 ml lemon juice
- 1 g dried oregano
- 15–30 ml water
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Put the olives, yoghurt, olive oil, lemon juice and oregano in a food processor.
2. Blend until creamy. Add water a little at a time until it reaches the desired consistency.
3. Taste before adding salt: the olives may already be salty enough. Finish with black pepper.

**Pairs with:** leafy greens, cucumber, eggs, chicken and roasted vegetables.
**Storage:** Keep refrigerated in a sealed container for up to 3 days.

## A final touch

Start with a small amount and add more to taste. If a sauce thickens in the refrigerator, stir in a few drops of water or lemon juice to loosen it. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and use clean utensils when serving.

With these 15 options, you can vary your meals with citrusy, creamy, herby and spicy combinations — without relying on ready-made sauces with added sugar.
