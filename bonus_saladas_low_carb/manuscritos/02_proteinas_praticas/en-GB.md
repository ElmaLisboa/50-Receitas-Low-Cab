# Protein Dishes for Low-Carb Meals

## 10 simple preparations to serve with salads and other dishes

This booklet brings together protein dishes that can be served with salads or used in other meals. They are standalone components, not complete salad recipes: prepare one option and pair it with the sides of your choice.

Each recipe serves two, unless stated otherwise. To make preparation easier, have the ingredients ready before you start and use a frying pan or baking tray that is the right size.

### Safe preparation and storage

- Keep raw meat, poultry, fish, seafood and eggs separate from ready-to-eat foods. Wash your hands and clean chopping boards and utensils after handling raw ingredients.
- When possible, check the internal temperature with a food thermometer: poultry, **74 °C**; minced meat, **71 °C**; whole cuts of beef or pork, **63 °C**, followed by a 3-minute rest; fish and seafood, **63 °C**. Egg dishes should be completely set.
- Put leftovers in clean, sealed containers and refrigerate them at **4 °C or below** within 2 hours of cooking. Eat them within 3 days; fish and seafood leftovers within 2 days. Reheat thoroughly before serving.

## 1. Grilled chicken with lemon, garlic and oregano

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 10 to 14 minutes

### Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast, divided into 2 fillets of similar thickness
- 10 ml olive oil
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 1 g finely grated lemon zest
- 3 g grated or finely chopped garlic
- 1 g dried oregano
- 2 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
2. Spread the mixture over the fillets. Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat.
3. Cook the chicken in the frying pan for about 5 to 7 minutes on each side, adjusting the time to the thickness of the fillets, until the centre reaches 74 °C.
4. Leave to rest for 3 minutes before slicing.

**Serving suggestion:** Slice and serve with leafy greens, cucumber, courgette or roasted vegetables.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## 2. Beef strips with paprika and rosemary

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 6 to 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 300 g rump or topside beef, cut into strips against the grain
- 10 ml olive oil
- 3 g finely chopped garlic
- 1 g smoked paprika
- 1 g chopped fresh rosemary or 0.5 g dried rosemary
- 5 ml red wine vinegar
- 1.5 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Pat the strips dry with kitchen paper. Season with salt, pepper, paprika and rosemary.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan until hot. Brown the beef in a single layer; if needed, cook it in two batches so it browns rather than stews in its own juices.
3. Turn the strips, add the garlic and cook until the beef is browned. Add the vinegar at the end and stir, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
4. If using a whole cut instead of strips, check that the thickest part reaches 63 °C and leave the meat to rest for 3 minutes before serving.

**Serving suggestion:** Pair with peppery leafy greens, grilled cabbage or sautéed mushrooms.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## 3. Baked salmon with lemon and dill

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 8 minutes

**Cooking time:** 10 to 15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets, about 150 g each
- 5 ml olive oil
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 2 g chopped fresh dill
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste
- 2 thin lemon slices, optional

### Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Line a small baking dish with baking parchment or lightly grease it.
2. Place the fillets in the dish. Season with olive oil, lemon juice, dill, salt and pepper. Add the lemon slices on top, if you like.
3. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, depending on thickness, until the fish is opaque and flakes easily. If using a thermometer, the centre should reach 63 °C.
4. Remove from the oven and serve straight away.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve the fillet whole or flaked with leafy greens, cucumber, broccoli or courgette.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 2 days.

## 4. Garlic and paprika prawns

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 5 to 7 minutes

### Ingredients
- 250 g raw prawns, peeled and deveined
- 10 ml olive oil
- 5 g finely chopped garlic
- 1 g sweet or smoked paprika
- 10 ml lemon juice
- 5 g chopped parsley
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Pat the prawns dry and season with salt, paprika and pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and stir for about 20 seconds, taking care not to burn it.
3. Arrange the prawns in a single layer. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes on each side, until pink, opaque and firm; the centre should reach 63 °C.
4. Turn off the heat, add the lemon juice and parsley, and stir.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve with leafy greens, cauliflower, courgette or sautéed cabbage.

**Storage:** After cooking, keep the prawns in a sealed container in the fridge and eat within 2 days.

## 5. Spinach and cheese mini frittatas — vegetarian

**Makes:** 2 portions (6 pieces, 3 per person)

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 15 to 20 minutes

### Ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 60 g fresh spinach, chopped
- 50 g grated mozzarella made with microbial or vegetable rennet
- 15 ml cream with no added sugar
- 5 ml olive oil, for greasing
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Grease 6 cups of a muffin tin with the olive oil.
2. In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the cream, salt and pepper.
3. Divide the spinach and mozzarella between the cups. Pour in the egg mixture until each cup is about three-quarters full.
4. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until the mini frittatas have risen and the centre is completely set. Leave to cool for 5 minutes before removing from the tin.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve warm or cold with leafy greens or grilled vegetables.

**Storage:** Keep the mini frittatas in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat thoroughly before serving.

## 6. Pork medallions with mustard and rosemary

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 12 to 16 minutes

### Ingredients
- 300 g pork fillet, cut into 4 medallions
- 10 ml olive oil
- 10 g mustard with no added sugar
- 3 g chopped garlic
- 1 g chopped fresh rosemary or 0.5 g dried rosemary
- 30 ml water
- 1.5 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Season the medallions with salt and pepper. Mix the mustard, garlic and rosemary, then spread over the meat.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Brown the medallions for about 2 minutes on each side.
3. Add the water, cover and cook over low heat, turning once, until the centre reaches 63 °C.
4. Remove from the heat and leave to rest for 3 minutes before serving.

**Serving suggestion:** Slice the medallions and serve with leafy greens, roasted cauliflower or grilled courgette.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## 7. Beef and Parmesan meatballs

**Makes:** 2 portions (8 meatballs)

**Preparation time:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 18 to 22 minutes

### Ingredients
- 300 g minced beef
- 1 egg
- 20 g grated Parmesan
- 3 g finely chopped garlic
- 1 g dried oregano
- 1.5 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste
- 5 ml olive oil, for greasing the baking tray

### Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C and lightly grease a baking tray.
2. In a bowl, mix the beef, egg, Parmesan, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper only until combined.
3. Divide into 8 portions and shape into meatballs with clean hands. Arrange them on the tray, leaving space between each one.
4. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, turning halfway through, until browned and the centre reaches 71 °C.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve with roasted vegetables, courgette ribbons or leafy greens.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## 8. Sesame and ginger tofu — vegan

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 10 to 12 minutes

### Ingredients
- 300 g firm tofu, drained
- 15 ml tamari with no added sugar
- 5 ml lemon juice
- 5 g freshly grated ginger
- 5 ml toasted sesame oil
- 10 ml olive oil or avocado oil, for the pan
- 5 g sesame seeds

### Method
1. Wrap the tofu in kitchen paper and place a light weight on top for 10 minutes to remove excess liquid. Cut into cubes or rectangles.
2. Mix the tamari, lemon juice, ginger and sesame oil. Coat the tofu in the mixture and leave to rest in the fridge for 10 minutes.
3. Heat the olive or avocado oil in a non-stick frying pan. Lift the pieces out of the marinade, let the excess drain off and brown them in a single layer, turning to brown all sides.
4. Add the remaining marinade and heat for 1 minute, stirring to coat the tofu well. Finish with the sesame seeds.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve with cabbage, cucumber, broccoli or sautéed vegetables.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

## 9. Grilled queijo coalho with oregano — vegetarian

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

**Cooking time:** 6 to 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 200 g queijo coalho made with a microbial or plant-based coagulant
- 1 g dried oregano
- 5 ml olive oil, if needed to grease the grill
- 1 g lemon zest, optional
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Cut the cheese into slices about 1.5 cm thick. Pat the surface lightly dry with kitchen paper.
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan or grill over medium heat. Grease with a little olive oil if needed.
3. Grill the cheese for 2 to 4 minutes on each side, until browned. Turn carefully so the slices stay whole.
4. Finish with oregano, pepper and lemon zest, if you like. Serve while still warm.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve with leafy greens, a small portion of tomato, courgette or grilled pepper.

**Storage:** The cheese is best grilled just before serving. If you have leftovers, keep them in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 2 days and reheat in a frying pan.

## 10. Portobello mushrooms stuffed with ricotta and spinach — vegetarian

**Makes:** 2 portions

**Preparation time:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** 18 to 22 minutes

### Ingredients
- 2 large Portobello mushrooms, about 250 g in total
- 100 g mashed ricotta made with a microbial or plant-based coagulant
- 40 g fresh spinach, chopped
- 15 g grated vegetarian Parmesan made with microbial or vegetable rennet
- 2 g chopped garlic
- 5 ml olive oil
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Wipe the mushrooms with damp kitchen paper. Remove the stems and, if you like, scrape out the dark gills with a small spoon.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan. Add the garlic and spinach and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until the spinach wilts. Remove from the heat.
3. Mix the spinach with the ricotta, Parmesan, salt and pepper.
4. Spoon the filling into the mushroom caps and place them on a baking tray. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until the mushrooms are tender and the filling is hot.

**Serving suggestion:** Serve as a vegetarian main or with leafy greens and grilled vegetables. If you cannot find Portobello mushrooms, use large mushrooms suitable for stuffing and adjust the cooking time to their size.

**Storage:** Keep leftovers in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat thoroughly before serving.
