# Low-Carb Snacks to Go

## 10 practical, tasty snacks that are easy to pack

Not every snack has to be complicated. This booklet brings together crunchy, creamy, savory, and sweet options made with simple ingredients and no added sugar. Each recipe makes individual portions that are easy to take to work, school, or on outings. You can also prepare them ahead and keep portions ready in the refrigerator.

Some options are dry and can travel in a container; others contain dairy or fresh ingredients and need to stay refrigerated. For perishable snacks, use an insulated cooler bag with a reusable ice pack and do not leave them out of the refrigerator for more than 2 hours. Check labels on processed meats, nut butters, plant-based beverages, and sweeteners for added sugar or starch.

## 1. Crispy Parmesan and Oregano Crisps

**Makes:** 2 servings (12 crisps)

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

**Cooking time:** 5 to 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 100 g finely grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 g dried oregano
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the Parmesan with the oregano and pepper. Make 12 small mounds, leaving space between them, and flatten each one slightly.
3. Bake for 5 to 8 minutes, watching closely, until the edges are golden.
4. Let the crisps cool completely on the baking sheet; they get crunchier as they cool.

**For taking along:** pack them in a rigid, dry container so they do not break. Store only after they are cool; moisture can soften them.

## 2. Almond, Flaxseed, and Rosemary Crackers

**Makes:** 2 servings (about 20 crackers)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** 18 to 22 minutes

### Ingredients
- 70 g almond flour
- 30 g ground flaxseed
- 10 g sesame seeds
- 15 ml olive oil
- 45 ml water
- 1 g dried rosemary
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the almond flour, flaxseed, sesame seeds, rosemary, and salt. Add the olive oil and water; stir until an even dough forms. If the dough is very dry, add more water gradually, 5 ml at a time.
3. Place the dough between two sheets of parchment paper and roll it out to about 2 mm thick.
4. Remove the top sheet, score squares with a knife, and transfer the dough to the baking sheet.
5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until golden around the edges. Let cool completely before separating the crackers.

**For taking along:** once cool, store in a dry, airtight container. If they lose their crunch, warm them in the oven for a few minutes and let them cool completely before storing again.

## 3. Crunchy Nut and Seed Mix

**Makes:** 2 servings

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

**Cooking time:** 12 to 15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 50 g unsalted almonds
- 30 g shelled walnuts
- 25 g pumpkin seeds
- 10 ml olive oil
- 1 g smoked paprika
- 1 g ground cumin
- 0.5 g red pepper flakes, optional
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 160 °C and line a small baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss the almonds, walnuts, and pumpkin seeds with the olive oil, paprika, cumin, pepper flakes, and salt.
3. Spread in one layer and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring once halfway through. Remove when fragrant and lightly golden; the nuts will keep toasting a little as they cool.
4. Let cool completely before dividing between two containers.

**For taking along:** pack in a tightly sealed container and keep away from heat and moisture. This mix contains nuts and seeds; it is not suitable for anyone allergic to these ingredients.

## 4. Turkey, Ricotta, and Cucumber Roll-Ups

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 roll-ups)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** no cooking

### Ingredients
- 120 g roasted turkey breast slices, with no added sugar
- 60 g mashed ricotta
- 40 g cucumber, cut into thin sticks
- 5 g chopped chives
- 5 ml lemon juice
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the ricotta with the chives, lemon juice, and pepper.
2. Place a small portion of the mixture on each turkey slice.
3. Add a few cucumber sticks and roll carefully. Secure each roll-up with a toothpick if needed.
4. Keep the roll-ups refrigerated until it is time to leave.

**For taking along:** pack in an insulated cooler bag with a reusable ice pack. Keep refrigerated and eat the same day; do not leave out of refrigeration for more than 2 hours.

## 5. Mini Bell Peppers Stuffed with Ricotta and Herbs

**Makes:** 2 servings (6 halves)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** no cooking

### Ingredients
- 3 mini bell peppers or 2 small bell peppers (about 180 g total)
- 80 g mashed ricotta
- 30 g plain full-fat unsweetened yogurt
- 5 g chopped parsley
- 5 g chopped chives
- 1 g lemon zest
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Wash and dry the peppers. Cut them in half lengthwise and remove the seeds and white ribs.
2. Mix the ricotta, yogurt, parsley, chives, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
3. Fill each half with the mixture and arrange in a lidded container.

**For taking along:** keep the stuffed peppers refrigerated and carry them in an insulated cooler bag with a reusable ice pack. Eat the same day.

## 6. Savory Almond and Cheese Bites

**Makes:** 2 servings (10 small bites)

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 12 to 15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 60 g almond flour
- 40 g shredded mozzarella
- 15 g grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg
- 1 g baking powder
- 1 g dried oregano
- 0.5 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the almond flour, mozzarella, Parmesan, baking powder, oregano, and salt.
3. Add the egg and stir until a moist dough forms. Divide into 10 portions and shape into small balls; flatten them slightly on the baking sheet.
4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until firm with golden edges. Let cool before storing.

**For taking along:** because they contain egg and cheese, keep them refrigerated in a sealed container and carry them in an insulated bag. Eat within 3 days.

## 7. No-Added-Sugar Cocoa and Coconut Truffles

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 truffles)

**Preparation time:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** no cooking

### Ingredients
- 50 g unsweetened shredded coconut
- 40 g unsweetened almond butter
- 10 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 15 ml unsweetened coconut cream
- 1 ml vanilla extract, optional
- Culinary sweetener with no sugar or maltodextrin, optional and to taste
- A pinch of salt

### Method
1. Mix the coconut, almond butter, cocoa, vanilla, and salt.
2. Gradually add the coconut cream, stirring until the mixture is moldable. If it is dry, add another 5 ml coconut cream.
3. Taste and, if desired, add a small amount of culinary sweetener. Mix again.
4. Divide into 8 portions and shape into balls. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up.

**For taking along:** keep refrigerated and carry in an insulated bag, especially on hot days. Eat within 3 days.

## 8. Strawberry and Vanilla Chia Pudding

**Makes:** 2 individual jars

**Preparation time:** 8 minutes, plus chilling time

**Cooking time:** no cooking

### Ingredients
- 20 g chia seeds
- 120 ml unsweetened almond milk
- 40 g plain unsweetened Greek yogurt
- 1 ml vanilla extract
- 40 g fresh strawberries, chopped
- Culinary sweetener with no sugar or maltodextrin, optional and to taste

### Method
1. Mix the chia seeds, almond milk, yogurt, and vanilla in a bowl.
2. Let stand for 5 minutes, then stir again to break up any lumps.
3. Divide into 2 lidded jars and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until the mixture has a pudding-like consistency.
4. If desired, sweeten the mixture before refrigerating. Add the chopped strawberries on top of each portion just before closing the jars.

**For taking along:** carry well chilled in an insulated cooler bag with a reusable ice pack. Keep refrigerated and eat within 2 days.

## 9. Peanut Butter and Cocoa Cookies

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 small cookies)

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 10 to 13 minutes

### Ingredients
- 50 g 100% peanut butter, unsweetened
- 40 g almond flour
- 1 egg
- 8 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 g baking powder
- 10 g erythritol, optional
- 1 ml vanilla extract, optional
- A pinch of salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the peanut butter, egg, vanilla, and erythritol, if using.
3. Add the almond flour, cocoa, baking powder, and salt. Mix until an even dough forms.
4. Divide into 8 portions, shape into balls, and flatten on the baking sheet.
5. Bake for 10 to 13 minutes. The cookies will still be soft when they come out of the oven; let them cool completely so they firm up.

**For taking along:** once cool, store in a sealed container. Since they contain egg, keep refrigerated and eat within 3 days.

## 10. Creamy Lemon Cream Cheese Cups

**Makes:** 2 individual cups

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes, plus chilling time

**Cooking time:** no cooking

### Ingredients
- 80 g cream cheese
- 60 g plain unsweetened Greek yogurt
- 8 ml lemon juice
- 1 g finely grated lemon zest
- 10 g powdered erythritol, optional
- 20 g chopped strawberries, optional, for topping

### Method
1. Let the cream cheese soften at room temperature for a few minutes, but do not leave it out for more than 2 hours.
2. Beat the cream cheese with the yogurt, lemon juice, and zest until smooth. Mix in the erythritol, if desired.
3. Divide between 2 small lidded cups and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
4. If using strawberries, add them on top shortly before closing the cups.

**For taking along:** keep the cups refrigerated and carry in an insulated cooler bag with a reusable ice pack. Eat within 2 days.

## Snacks Ready for Your Routine

To make packing easier, divide the snacks into individual portions and choose packaging based on texture: rigid containers for crunchy items and lidded jars for creamy options. Perishable snacks should stay chilled until they are ready to eat.
