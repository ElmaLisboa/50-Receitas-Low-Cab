# Low-Carb Snacks to Take with You

## 10 practical, tasty snacks that travel well

Not every snack has to be complicated. This booklet brings together crunchy, creamy, savoury and sweet options made with simple ingredients and no added sugar. Each recipe makes individual portions that are easy to take to work, school or on outings. You can also prepare them ahead and keep the portions ready in the fridge.

Some options are dry and can be carried in a container; others contain dairy or fresh ingredients and must be kept chilled. For perishable snacks, use an insulated bag with a reusable ice pack and do not leave them out of the fridge for more than 2 hours. Check the labels on processed meats, nut butters, plant-based drinks and sweeteners for added sugar or starch.

## 1. Crispy Parmesan and Oregano Crisps

**Makes:** 2 servings (12 crisps)

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

**Cooking time:** 5 to 8 minutes

### Ingredients
- 100 g finely grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 g dried oregano
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Mix the Parmesan with the oregano and pepper. Make 12 small mounds, leaving space between them, and flatten each one slightly.
3. Bake for 5 to 8 minutes, keeping a close eye on them, until the edges are golden.
4. Leave to cool completely on the tray; the crisps become crunchier as they cool.

**Packing tip:** carry them in a rigid, dry container so they do not break. Store them only once cool; moisture can soften them.

## 2. Almond, Flaxseed and Rosemary Crackers

**Makes:** 2 servings (about 20 crackers)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** 18 to 22 minutes

### Ingredients
- 70 g almond flour
- 30 g ground flaxseed
- 10 g sesame seeds
- 15 ml olive oil
- 45 ml water
- 1 g dried rosemary
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Mix the almond flour, ground flaxseed, sesame, rosemary and salt. Add the olive oil and water; stir until an even dough forms. If it is very dry, add more water gradually, 5 ml at a time.
3. Place the dough between two sheets of baking parchment and roll it out to about 2 mm thick.
4. Remove the top sheet, score squares with a knife and transfer the dough to the baking tray.
5. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until golden at the edges. Leave to cool completely before separating the crackers.

**Packing tip:** once cool, store in a dry, airtight container. If the crackers lose their crunch, warm them in the oven for a few minutes and let them cool completely before storing again.

## 3. Crunchy Nut and Seed Mix

**Makes:** 2 servings

**Preparation time:** 5 minutes

**Cooking time:** 12 to 15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 50 g unsalted almonds
- 30 g shelled walnuts
- 25 g pumpkin seeds
- 10 ml olive oil
- 1 g smoked paprika
- 1 g ground cumin
- 0.5 g chilli flakes, optional
- 1 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 160 °C and line a small baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Toss the almonds, walnuts and pumpkin seeds with the olive oil, paprika, cumin, chilli and salt.
3. Spread in a single layer and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring once halfway through. Remove when aromatic and lightly golden; the nuts will continue to toast a little as they cool.
4. Leave to cool completely before dividing between two containers.

**Packing tip:** carry in a tightly sealed container and keep away from heat and moisture. This mix contains nuts and seeds; it is not suitable for anyone allergic to these ingredients.

## 4. Turkey, Ricotta and Cucumber Roll-Ups

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 roll-ups)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** No cooking required

### Ingredients
- 120 g sliced roast turkey breast, with no added sugar
- 60 g mashed ricotta
- 40 g cucumber, cut into thin sticks
- 5 g chopped chives
- 5 ml lemon juice
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the ricotta with the chives, lemon juice and pepper.
2. Place a small portion of the mixture on each turkey slice.
3. Add a few cucumber sticks and roll up carefully. Secure each roll-up with a cocktail stick if needed.
4. Keep the roll-ups chilled until you leave.

**Packing tip:** carry in an insulated bag with a reusable ice pack. Keep chilled and eat the same day; do not leave out of refrigeration for more than 2 hours.

## 5. Mini Peppers Stuffed with Ricotta and Herbs

**Makes:** 2 servings (6 halves)

**Preparation time:** 12 minutes

**Cooking time:** No cooking required

### Ingredients
- 3 mini peppers or 2 small sweet peppers (about 180 g in total)
- 80 g mashed ricotta
- 30 g plain full-fat yoghurt, unsweetened
- 5 g chopped parsley
- 5 g chopped chives
- 1 g finely grated lemon zest
- 1 g salt
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. Wash and dry the peppers. Cut them in half lengthways and remove the seeds and white ribs.
2. Mix the ricotta, yoghurt, parsley, chives, lemon zest, salt and pepper.
3. Fill each half with the mixture and place in a lidded container.

**Packing tip:** keep the stuffed peppers chilled and carry them in an insulated bag with a reusable ice pack. Eat the same day.

## 6. Savoury Almond and Cheese Bites

**Makes:** 2 servings (10 small bites)

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 12 to 15 minutes

### Ingredients
- 60 g almond flour
- 40 g grated mozzarella
- 15 g grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 egg
- 1 g baking powder
- 1 g dried oregano
- 0.5 g salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Mix the almond flour, mozzarella, Parmesan, baking powder, oregano and salt.
3. Add the egg and stir to form a moist dough. Divide into 10 portions and roll into small balls; flatten them slightly on the tray.
4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until firm with golden edges. Leave to cool before storing.

**Packing tip:** because they contain egg and cheese, keep them chilled in a sealed container and carry them in an insulated bag. Eat within 3 days.

## 7. Cocoa and Coconut Truffles with No Added Sugar

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 truffles)

**Preparation time:** 15 minutes

**Cooking time:** No cooking required

### Ingredients
- 50 g unsweetened desiccated coconut
- 40 g unsweetened almond butter
- 10 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 15 ml unsweetened coconut cream
- 1 ml vanilla extract, optional
- A sweetener suitable for cooking, with no sugar or maltodextrin; optional, to taste
- A pinch of salt

### Method
1. Mix the coconut, almond butter, cocoa, vanilla and salt.
2. Gradually add the coconut cream, stirring until the mixture can be shaped. If it is dry, add another 5 ml of coconut cream.
3. Taste and, if you like, add a small amount of the sweetener. Mix again.
4. Divide into 8 portions and shape into balls. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up.

**Packing tip:** keep chilled and carry in an insulated bag, especially on hot days. Eat within 3 days.

## 8. Strawberry and Vanilla Chia Pudding

**Makes:** 2 individual pots

**Preparation time:** 8 minutes, plus chilling time

**Cooking time:** No cooking required

### Ingredients
- 20 g chia seeds
- 120 ml unsweetened almond drink
- 40 g plain unsweetened Greek yoghurt
- 1 ml vanilla extract
- 40 g fresh strawberries, chopped
- A sweetener suitable for cooking, with no sugar or maltodextrin; optional, to taste

### Method
1. Mix the chia seeds, almond drink, yoghurt and vanilla in a bowl.
2. Leave to stand for 5 minutes, then stir again to break up any lumps.
3. Divide between two lidded pots and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until the mixture has a pudding-like consistency.
4. If you like, add the sweetener to the mixture before chilling. Add the chopped strawberries on top of each portion just before closing the pots.

**Packing tip:** carry well chilled in an insulated bag with a reusable ice pack. Keep refrigerated and eat within 2 days.

## 9. Peanut Butter and Cocoa Cookies

**Makes:** 2 servings (8 small cookies)

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes

**Cooking time:** 10 to 13 minutes

### Ingredients
- 50 g 100% peanut butter, unsweetened
- 40 g almond flour
- 1 egg
- 8 g unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 g baking powder
- 10 g erythritol, optional
- 1 ml vanilla extract, optional
- A pinch of salt

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Mix the peanut butter, egg, vanilla and erythritol, if using.
3. Add the almond flour, cocoa, baking powder and salt. Mix until an even dough forms.
4. Divide into 8 portions, roll into balls and flatten them on the tray.
5. Bake for 10 to 13 minutes. The cookies will still be soft when they come out of the oven; leave to cool completely so they firm up.

**Packing tip:** once cool, store in a sealed container. As they contain egg, keep chilled and eat within 3 days.

## 10. Creamy Lemon and Cream Cheese Pots

**Makes:** 2 individual pots

**Preparation time:** 10 minutes, plus chilling time

**Cooking time:** No cooking required

### Ingredients
- 80 g cream cheese
- 60 g plain unsweetened Greek yoghurt
- 8 ml lemon juice
- 1 g finely grated lemon zest
- 10 g powdered erythritol, optional
- 20 g chopped strawberries, optional, to finish

### Method
1. Leave the cream cheese out of the fridge for a few minutes to soften, but do not leave it at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
2. Beat the cream cheese with the yoghurt, lemon juice and zest until smooth. Stir in the erythritol, if you like.
3. Divide between two small lidded pots and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
4. If using strawberries, add them on top just before closing the pots.

**Packing tip:** keep the pots chilled and carry them in an insulated bag with a reusable ice pack. Eat within 2 days.

## Snacks Ready for Your Day

To make packing easier, divide the snacks into individual portions and choose packaging to suit their texture: rigid containers for crunchy items and lidded pots for creamy options. Perishable snacks should stay chilled until they are ready to eat.
