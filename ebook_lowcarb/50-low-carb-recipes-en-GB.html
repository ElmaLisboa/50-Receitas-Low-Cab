Welcome to Your Low-Carb Kitchen

You did not buy a diet book. You bought 50 ready-to-use answers to the question that can derail any eating routine: “What should I cook today?”.

Low carb does not mean living on sad salads. It means enjoying a creamy omelette, a lasagne you can slice, a Sunday feijoada, and even cheesecake — swapping refined flour and sugar for ingredients that satisfy, without cooking two separate meals.

Every recipe was selected for three reasons: easy-to-find ingredients, realistic time for busy people, and leftovers that solve tomorrow’s meal.

The golden rule of this book: carbohydrates are accents, not the foundation. They add flavour and colour — tomato, carrot, fruit — while the foundation is always protein, good fat and vegetables.

Cook one new recipe a day for seven weeks and you will have a repertoire that can rescue any Monday. Enjoy.

How to Use This Book

1. Start at the end. Check the “7-Day Menu” bonus and the shopping list before you go to the shops. Cooking without a plan is the fastest way to order a takeaway on Thursday.

2. Double the right recipe. Most lunch and dinner recipes make two servings: the second becomes tomorrow’s lunch.

3. Use the boxes. Each recipe includes a Cooking Tip and a Smart Swap to help you adapt without leaving the low-carb approach.

4. Do not chase zero carbs. The nutritional numbers are there to help you choose, not to turn food into a prison.

5. Freeze with confidence. Recipes with freezing notes were designed for individual portions and sealed containers.

Start the day with practical, satisfying recipes that do not rely on traditional bread. This chapter brings together eggs, cheese, almond or coconut flour and fruit in balanced portions. Most options require up to 15 minutes of active preparation.

01

Baked Spinach and Cheese Omelette

Preparation 10 minTotal 30 minServes 2Difficulty easy
385kcal / serving6 gcarbohydrates27 gprotein28 gfat

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup chopped spinach (fresh or frozen, squeezed dry)
  • 50 g grated mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp double cream
  • 1/2 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, black pepper and nutmeg to taste

Method

  1. Sauté the onion in olive oil for 3 minutes, add the spinach and cook until the water evaporates.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the cream, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  3. Fold the sautéed mixture and the cheese into the eggs.
  4. Pour into a small greased baking dish and bake at 180 °C for 20–25 minutes, until the centre is set.
COOKING TIP: Make two at once: the second one keeps 3 days in the fridge and becomes tomorrow's breakfast.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the spinach for grated courgette squeezed dry — same technique, milder flavour.
02

2-Minute Low-Carb Microwave Bread

Preparation 5 minTotal 7 minServes 1Difficulty easy
245kcal / serving4 gcarbohydrates14 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp almond flour
  • 1 tbsp double cream
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1/2 tsp butter for greasing

Method

  1. Mix everything in a mug or small greased square dish with a fork until no lumps of flour remain.
  2. Microwave on high for 90 seconds.
  3. Turn out, cut in half and toast in a pan or toaster for 1 minute on each side.
COOKING TIP: It is only good if you toast it: the microwave cooks the bread but gives it no colour and no crumb.
↺ SMART SWAP: No almond flour? Use 1 tbsp golden flaxseed meal and 1 tbsp coconut flour.
03

Banana Oat Pancakes (No Flour)

Preparation 8 minTotal 15 minServes 2Difficulty easy
260kcal / serving22 gcarbohydrates15 gprotein12 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 very ripe banana
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp oat bran
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp butter or coconut oil

Method

  1. Mash the banana with a fork until smooth.
  2. Add the eggs, oats, cinnamon and baking powder and mix well.
  3. Heat the butter over low heat and drop small ladles of batter.
  4. Cook 2 minutes on each side, flipping as soon as bubbles appear on the surface.
COOKING TIP: The banana should be heavily speckled: that is what sweetens the batter with no added sugar.
↺ SMART SWAP: Replace the banana with 1/2 grated apple and 1 tsp sweetener to cut the carbs further.

Savoury Egg, Vegetable and Bacon Muffins

Preparation 15 minTotal 35 minServes 6Difficulty easy
180kcal / serving3 gcarbohydrates12 gprotein13 gfat

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1/2 cup diced bacon
  • 1/2 courgette, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives
  • salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Brown the bacon and drain on kitchen paper.
  2. Grate the courgette, salt it, wait 5 minutes and squeeze it firmly in your hands.
  3. Whisk the eggs, fold everything together and divide among the cups of a muffin tin.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 20–25 minutes, until firm in the centre.
COOKING TIP: Freeze in a resealable bag and microwave 30 seconds: breakfast is ready in half a minute.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the bacon for shredded chicken for a version lower in saturated fat.

Stovetop Tapioca Crepe with Cheese and Tomato

Preparation 5 minTotal 10 minServes 1Difficulty easy
290kcal / serving14 gcarbohydrates18 gprotein16 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 level tbsp tapioca starch
  • 1 tbsp cream cheese or double cream
  • 2 slices mozzarella
  • 3 thin tomato slices
  • oregano and salt to taste

Method

  1. Whisk the egg with the tapioca, cream cheese and salt until smooth.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over low heat and pour in the batter.
  3. Cook 2 minutes, flip, top with cheese and tomato and fold in half.
  4. Leave 1 more minute covered so the cheese melts.
COOKING TIP: Low heat is the secret: on high heat the crepe dries out and cracks when you fold it.
↺ SMART SWAP: Sweet version: fill with 1 tsp peanut butter and cinnamon.

Stovetop Cheese Bread

Preparation 5 minTotal 10 minServes 1Difficulty easy
275kcal / serving6 gcarbohydrates16 gprotein21 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp sour cassava starch
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan or mozzarella
  • 1 tbsp double cream
  • 1 pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix everything with a fork until you have a creamy batter.
  2. Pour into a small greased non-stick pan over low heat.
  3. Cover and cook 4 minutes, flip and cook 3 minutes more.
  4. Cut in half and fill while hot.
COOKING TIP: Use sour (fermented) cassava starch, not the sweet kind: the sour one gives the characteristic chew.
↺ SMART SWAP: Go-to filling: cream cheese and oregano, or shredded chicken with cream cheese.

Creamy Chicken Collard Wrap

Preparation 12 minTotal 20 minServes 2Difficulty easy
310kcal / serving9 gcarbohydrates26 gprotein19 gfat

Ingredients

  • 4 large collard green leaves
  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicken
  • 3 tbsp cream cheese or natural yoghurt
  • 1/2 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 cup chopped lettuce
  • juice of 1/4 lemon, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Blanch the collard leaves 20 seconds in boiling water, then in ice water: they soften without cooking.
  2. Mix the chicken with the cream cheese, lemon, salt and pepper.
  3. Assemble on the dried leaf: lettuce, carrot and creamy chicken.
  4. Roll tightly like a cigar, folding in the ends, and cut in half.
COOKING TIP: Pat the collard dry with kitchen paper: a wet leaf makes the wrap fall apart in your hand.
↺ SMART SWAP: Use raw chard leaves instead — crunchier and no blanching needed.
08

Creamy Scrambled Eggs with Avocado and Seeds

Preparation 8 minTotal 10 minServes 1Difficulty easy
405kcal / serving8 gcarbohydrates20 gprotein33 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 small avocado, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sunflower or pumpkin seeds
  • chopped chives, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper without whipping them foamy.
  2. Melt the butter over low heat, add the eggs and wait 20 seconds without stirring.
  3. Pull a spatula from the centre to the edges, repeating until creamy and still wet.
  4. Serve immediately topped with avocado, seeds and chives.
COOKING TIP: Take them off the heat while they still look underdone: the pan's residual heat finishes them.
↺ SMART SWAP: No avocado? Use 2 tbsp sour cream with lemon.
09

Protein Yoghurt with Nut Granola

Preparation 5 minTotal 5 minServes 1Difficulty easy
320kcal / serving10 gcarbohydrates22 gprotein22 gfat

Ingredients

  • 170 g whole-milk natural yoghurt
  • 1/3 cup chopped mixed nuts (Brazil nuts, cashews and almonds)
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened coconut flakes
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp sweetener or honey (optional)
  • 5 strawberries, chopped

Method

  1. Toast the nuts and coconut flakes in a dry pan for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
  2. Spoon the yoghurt into a bowl and top with the strawberries.
  3. Scatter the warm granola over and dust with cinnamon.
  4. Sweeten only if needed: the cinnamon already fools the palate.
COOKING TIP: Never toast granola with your phone in reach: nuts burn in 30 seconds and turn bitter.
↺ SMART SWAP: Batch it: triple the granola and keep it 15 days in a sealed jar.

10

Simple Cauliflower Rice

Preparation 10 minTotal 15 minServes 4Difficulty easy
65kcal / serving5 gcarbohydrates4 gprotein3 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, in florets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 onion, chopped
  • salt and fresh herbs

Method

  1. Pulse the raw cauliflower until rice-sized, or grate it on the largest holes.
  2. Sauté the garlic and onion in olive oil for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the cauliflower and stir-fry over high heat for 5–6 minutes.
  4. Salt and finish with fresh herbs — do not cover, or it turns to mash.
COOKING TIP: Never process cooked cauliflower: it releases water and turns to paste. Always raw, and pulse.
↺ SMART SWAP: A neutral base: it keeps 4 days in the fridge and takes any leftover protein on top.
11

Courgette Noodles with Homemade Tomato Sauce

Preparation 12 minTotal 30 minServes 2Difficulty easy
265kcal / serving14 gcarbohydrates12 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 large courgettes
  • 6 ripe tomatoes (or 1 tin peeled whole tomatoes)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • basil, salt and pepper
  • grated parmesan to finish

Method

  1. Make the sauce: sauté onion and garlic, add chopped tomato and simmer 20 minutes until thick.
  2. Slice the courgette into thin ribbons with a peeler or spiraliser.
  3. Sauté the courgette 90 seconds in hot oil — just to warm it, never to cook it.
  4. Add the sauce, toss briefly and finish with basil and parmesan.
COOKING TIP: Salt the spiralised courgette, drain 10 minutes in a colander and pat dry: that removes the water that dilutes the sauce.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the peeled tomatoes for a sauce of blended cauliflower with parmesan — lower in carbs still.

Garlic and Oil Palm Heart Noodles

Preparation 10 minTotal 20 minServes 2Difficulty easy
210kcal / serving7 gcarbohydrates8 gprotein17 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 jar shredded hearts of palm (or konjac spaghetti)
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • parsley, black pepper and salt

Method

  1. Drain the hearts of palm and pat them very dry with kitchen paper.
  2. Warm the olive oil over low heat and golden the sliced garlic without burning it.
  3. Add the hearts of palm and sauté 3 minutes over medium-high heat to drive off water.
  4. Turn off the heat, toss with parmesan, parsley and pepper and serve at once.
COOKING TIP: Hearts of palm and konjac release a lot of water: drying them well before sautéing is what keeps the dish from turning soggy.
↺ SMART SWAP: Add 1 cup sautéed prawns to make it a complete main.

Complete Chicken Salad with Yoghurt Dressing

Preparation 15 minTotal 25 minServes 2Difficulty easy
340kcal / serving12 gcarbohydrates32 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 grilled chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 4 cups mixed salad leaves
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced into rounds
  • 1 tomato, cut into wedges
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 170 g natural yoghurt
  • 1 tsp mustard, juice of 1/2 lemon, olive oil, salt and herbs

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and paprika and pan-fry 5 minutes on each side.
  2. Divide the greens, cucumber, tomato and avocado between two bowls.
  3. Whisk the yoghurt with mustard, lemon, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and herbs.
  4. Top with the warm chicken and dress right before eating.
COOKING TIP: Dressing on the side keeps a salad 2 days in a container; dressed ahead, it wilts in 3 hours.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the chicken for tuna or cooked chickpeas (increase the portion and count the carbs).

Tuna Salad with Egg and Lemon Mayonnaise

Preparation 12 minTotal 20 minServes 2Difficulty easy
375kcal / serving6 gcarbohydrates28 gprotein27 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 tins tuna in water, drained
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 1/4 red onion, chopped
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 1 tsp mustard, salt, pepper and parsley

Method

  1. Mix the mayonnaise with lemon, mustard, salt and pepper.

COOKING TIP: Celery is what gives the crunch: without it the salad turns pasty.
↺ SMART SWAP: Grab-and-go: layer it in a jar with the greens at the bottom and the mix on top.

Spicy Beef Mince Lettuce Wrap

Preparation 15 minTotal 25 minServes 3Difficulty easy
320kcal / serving8 gcarbohydrates26 gprotein21 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 iceberg lettuce (whole leaves)
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 small pepper, chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • smoked paprika, cumin, pepper and salt
  • grated cheese and sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Sauté the onion, garlic and pepper for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the beef mince and brown well, breaking it up with a spatula.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce and spices and cook 8 minutes until the liquid is gone.
  4. Serve in lettuce leaves with cheese and sour cream.
COOKING TIP: Pat the lettuce leaves dry: a wet leaf tears when you roll it.
↺ SMART SWAP: No beef mince? Use shredded chicken with the same spices.

Cauliflower Shepherd's Pie with Shredded Beef

Preparation 25 minTotal 60 minServes 6Difficulty easy
390kcal / serving13 gcarbohydrates28 gprotein25 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower
  • 400 g salted dried beef, desalted and shredded
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 100 g grated mozzarella
  • fresh herbs

Method

  1. Steam the cauliflower until tender and blend with butter and cream cheese until smooth.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic, add the shredded dried beef and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Layer in a baking dish: meat on the bottom, mash on top, mozzarella last.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 20 minutes until golden.
COOKING TIP: Drain the cooked cauliflower well before blending: excess water makes the mash runny.
↺ SMART SWAP: No dried beef? Use beef mince sautéed with pepper, or shredded chicken.

Aubergine Lasagne Bolognese

Preparation 30 minTotal 75 minServes 6Difficulty easy
355kcal / serving12 gcarbohydrates30 gprotein22 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 large aubergines, in thin slices
  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 tin peeled whole tomatoes
  • 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves
  • 200 g sliced mozzarella
  • 100 g grated parmesan
  • olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Salt the aubergine slices, rest 20 minutes, rinse and pat dry.
  2. Fry them in a hot pan with a little olive oil, 2 minutes on each side.
  3. Make the sauce: sauté onion and garlic, brown the beef, add tomato and simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Layer sauce, aubergine and mozzarella, ending with sauce and parmesan. Bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes.
COOKING TIP: Drying the aubergine is mandatory: skip it and the lasagne releases water and turns to soup in the dish.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the aubergine for thin courgette slices and fry them for less time.

Stuffed Aubergine with Beef Mince

Preparation 20 minTotal 50 minServes 4Difficulty easy
330kcal / serving11 gcarbohydrates26 gprotein20 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 aubergines, halved
  • 400 g beef mince
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup grated mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • parsley, salt, pepper and cumin

Method

  1. Scoop out some of the aubergine flesh, chop it and set aside.
  2. Salt the shells and bake at 180 °C for 15 minutes to soften.
  3. Sauté onion and garlic, brown the meat, add the chopped flesh and tomato and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Fill the shells, top with cheese and return to the oven 15 minutes.
COOKING TIP: Bake the shells before filling: raw aubergine in the oven stays hard inside and burnt outside.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the beef mince for tuna and cream cheese for a 25-minute version.

Low-Carb Kale Soup (No Potato)

Preparation 15 minTotal 35 minServes 4Difficulty easy
195kcal / serving9 gcarbohydrates14 gprotein12 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 small cauliflower, in florets
  • 200 g Portuguese sausage or calabresa, sliced into rounds
  • 1 bunch collard greens, in thin strips
  • 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 L chicken stock or water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the sausage in a large pot and reserve some of the fat.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic in that fat for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the cauliflower and stock, cook 15 minutes and blend until creamy.
  4. Return to the pot, add the sliced collard and sausage and cook 5 minutes without letting the greens turn limp.
COOKING TIP: Collard goes in last and cooks only 5 minutes: it should stay bright green, not brown.
↺ SMART SWAP: Cured-meat-free version: swap the sausage for shredded chicken and add smoked paprika.

Pumpkin Soup with Coconut Milk and Ginger

Preparation 15 minTotal 40 minServes 4Difficulty easy
180kcal / serving16 gcarbohydrates5 gprotein11 gfat

Ingredients

  • 500 g kabocha squash, diced
  • 200 ml unsweetened coconut milk
  • 1 (3 cm) piece of ginger, grated
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 700 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper and chives

Method

  1. Sauté the onion, garlic and ginger in olive oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the squash and stock and cook 20 minutes until tender.
  3. Blend with the coconut milk until smooth.
  4. Adjust the salt, rewarm without boiling and finish with chives.
COOKING TIP: Kabocha has more carbs than other vegetables: mind your portion if you are in the induction phase.
↺ SMART SWAP: Swap the ginger for curry powder and finish with coriander for a Thai profile.

Vegetable Soup with Shredded Chicken

Preparation 20 minTotal 45 minServes 4Difficulty easy
240kcal / serving14 gcarbohydrates24 gprotein10 gfat

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken breast
  • 1 courgette, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 cup chopped green beans
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves and 1 tomato
  • 1.2 L water or stock, olive oil, salt, bay leaf and parsley

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in water with bay leaf and salt 20 minutes, remove and shred.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic separately and return them to the pot with the tomato.
  3. Add the carrot and cook 8 minutes; then the courgette, green beans and cauliflower for 8 more.
  4. Stir in the shredded chicken, adjust the salt and finish with parsley.
COOKING TIP: Cook the vegetables in order of firmness: carrot first, leafy greens and courgette last.
↺ SMART SWAP: Freeze in 500 ml jars: it keeps 3 months and rescues an unplanned dinner.

Creamy Courgette Soup with Cream Cheese

Preparation 12 minTotal 30 minServes 3Difficulty easy
175kcal / serving8 gcarbohydrates8 gprotein13 gfat

Ingredients

  • 3 large courgettes, sliced into rounds
  • 100 g cream cheese
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 600 ml vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, white pepper and nutmeg

Method

23

Pan-Fried Chicken with Mustard Cream Sauce

Preparation 12 minTotal 25 minServes 2Difficulty easy
385kcal / serving5 gcarbohydrates38 gprotein24 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breast fillets (150 g each)
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 100 ml double cream
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Season the fillets with salt and pepper and sear in a very hot pan, 4 minutes on each side. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan, melt the butter and sauté the garlic 30 seconds.
  3. Add the mustard, cream and thyme and reduce 3 minutes until thickened.
  4. Return the chicken to the pan, coat in the sauce and serve with sautéed vegetables.
COOKING TIP: Do not wash the pan between chicken and sauce: the browned bits are what flavour the cream base.
↺ SMART SWAP: No Dijon? Use mild mustard with 1/2 tsp honey or sweetener to balance the acidity.
24

Oven-Roasted Chicken Thighs with Lemon and Rosemary

Preparation 10 minTotal 50 minServes 4Difficulty easy
410kcal / serving3 gcarbohydrates36 gprotein28 gfat

Ingredients

  • 8 skin-on chicken thighs
  • juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 4 rosemary sprigs
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • coarse salt and black pepper
