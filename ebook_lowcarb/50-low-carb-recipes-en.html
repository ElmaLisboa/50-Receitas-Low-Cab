Welcome to Your Low-Carb Kitchen

You did not buy a diet book. You bought 50 ready-to-use answers to the question that can derail any eating routine: “What should I cook today?”.

Low carb does not mean living on sad salads. It means enjoying a creamy omelet, a lasagna you can slice, a Sunday feijoada, and even cheesecake — swapping refined flour and sugar for ingredients that satisfy, without cooking two separate meals.

Every recipe was selected for three reasons: easy-to-find ingredients, realistic time for busy people, and leftovers that solve tomorrow’s meal.

The golden rule of this book: carbohydrates are accents, not the foundation. They add flavor and color — tomato, carrot, fruit — while the foundation is always protein, good fat, and vegetables.

Cook one new recipe a day for seven weeks and you will have a repertoire that can rescue any Monday. Enjoy.

How to Use This Book

1. Start at the end. Check the “7-Day Menu” bonus and the shopping list before going to the store. Cooking without a plan is the fastest way to order takeout on Thursday.

2. Double the right recipe. Most lunch and dinner recipes make two servings: the second becomes tomorrow’s lunch.

3. Use the boxes. Each recipe includes a Cooking Tip and a Smart Swap to help you adapt without leaving the low-carb approach.

4. Do not chase zero carbs. The nutritional numbers are there to help you choose, not to turn food into a prison.

5. Freeze with confidence. Recipes with freezing notes were designed for individual portions and sealed containers.

01

Baked Spinach and Cheese Omelet

Preparation 10 minTotal 30 minYield 2Difficulty easy
385kcal / serving6 gcarbohydrates27 gprotein28 gfat

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup chopped spinach (fresh or frozen, squeezed)
  • 50 g of grated mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tbsp of heavy cream
  • 1/2 onion chopped
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil
  • salt, black pepper and nutmeg to taste

Method

  1. Sauté the onion in the olive oil for 3 minutes, add the spinach and cook until the water evaporates.
  2. Beat the eggs with the heavy cream, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  3. Mix the sautéed mixture and cheese into the eggs.
  4. Pour in a small greased baking dish and bake at 180 °C for 20-25 minutes, until the center sets.
COOKING TIP: Make two at once: the second lasts 3 days in the refrigerator and becomes tomorrow's breakfast.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the spinach for grated and squeezed zucchini — same technique, flavor more neutral.

2-Minute Low-Carb Microwave Bread

Preparation 5 minTotal 7 minYield 1Difficulty easy
245kcal / serving4 gcarbohydrates14 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp of almond flour
  • 1 tbsp of heavy cream
  • 1/2 tsp of baking powder
  • 1 pitada of salt
  • 1/2 tsp of manteiga para untar

Method

  1. Mix tudo in a mug or container quadrado greased, with a garfo, until sumirem the bolinhas of flour.
  2. Microwave for 90 segundos in potência alta.
  3. Desenforme, cut to the meio and brown in the frigideira or torradeira for 1 minute of cada lado.
COOKING TIP: Só is bom se você brown then — the micro-ondas assa, mas não dá cor nem textura of pão.
↺ SMART SWAP: without almond flour? Use 1 tbsp of golden flaxseed meal + 1 of coco.

Banana Oat Pancakes (No Flour)

Preparation 8 minTotal 15 minYield 2Difficulty easy
260kcal / serving22 gcarbohydrates15 gprotein12 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 banana bem madura
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp of farelo of aveia
  • 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp of fermento
  • 1 tsp of manteiga or oil of coco

Method

  1. Mash the banana with a garfo until become purê.
  2. Add the eggs, the aveia, the cinnamon and the fermento and mix bem.
  3. Heat the manteiga in low heat and pour conchas pequenas.
  4. Brown 2 minutes of cada lado, virando quando aparecerem bolhas in the superfície.
COOKING TIP: A banana precisa estar with the casca pintada of marrom: is the que adoça unsweetened.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the banana for 1/2 maçã grated + 1 tsp of sweetener para reduzir still more the carbo.

Savory Egg, Vegetable and Bacon Muffins

Preparation 15 minTotal 35 minYield 6Difficulty easy
180kcal / serving3 gcarbohydrates12 gprotein13 gfat

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1/2 cups of bacon diced
  • 1/2 grated and squeezed zucchini
  • 1/2 cups of cheese grated
  • 2 tbsp of scallions chopped
  • salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Fry the bacon until brown and drain in papel-toalha.
  2. Grate the zucchini, salt, espere 5 minutes and squeeze bem with the mãos.
  3. Beat the eggs, mix todos the ingredientes and divide in forminhas of muffin.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 20-25 minutes, until the center become firme.
COOKING TIP: Congele in saco zip and reaqueça 30 segundos in the micro-ondas: café pronto in meio minute.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the bacon for shredded chicken para a version with menos gordura saturada.

Skillet Tapioca Crepe with Cheese and Tomato

Preparation 5 minTotal 10 minYield 1Difficulty easy
290kcal / serving14 gcarbohydrates18 gprotein16 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp rasa of tapioca gum
  • 1 tbsp of cream cheese or heavy cream
  • 2 fatias of cheese mozzarella
  • 3 rodelas finas of tomato
  • oregano and salt to taste

Method

  1. Beat the egg with the tapioca, the cream cheese and the salt until become homogêneo.
  2. Heat a frigideira antiaderente in low heat and pour the mistura.
  3. Cook 2 minutes, vire, cover with cheese and tomato and dobre to the meio.
  4. Let more 1 minute with the tampa fechada para the cheese derreter.
COOKING TIP: heat baixo is the segredo: in high heat the crepioca resseca and racha in the hora of dobrar.
↺ SMART SWAP: version doce: fill with 1 tbsp of chá of peanut butter and cinnamon.

Creamy Chicken Collard Wrap

Preparation 12 minTotal 20 minYield 2Difficulty easy
310kcal / serving9 gcarbohydrates26 gprotein19 gfat

Ingredients

  • 4 folhas grandes of collard greens
  • 1 cup of frango cooked shredded
  • 3 tbsp of cream cheese or yogurt natural
  • 1/2 carrot grated
  • 1/2 cups of alface chopped
  • juice of 1/4 of limão, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Coat the folhas of kale for water boiling for 20 segundos and then in water gelada — isso amacia without cozinhar.
  2. Mix the frango with the cream cheese, the limão, salt and pepper.
  3. Assemble sobre the folha seca: alface, carrot and the frango creamy.
  4. Enrole firme como a charuto, dobrando the laterais, and cut to the meio.
COOKING TIP: Seque bem the kale with papel-toalha: folha molhada faz the wrap desmontar in the mão.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the kale for folha of acelga raw, que is more crispy and não precisa of choque térmico.

Creamy Scrambled Eggs with Avocado and Seeds

Preparation 8 minTotal 10 minYield 1Difficulty easy
405kcal / serving8 gcarbohydrates20 gprotein33 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp of manteiga
  • 1/2 avocado small sliced
  • 1 tbsp of sunflower seeds or pumpkin
  • scallions chopped, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper without fazer espuma.
  2. Melt the manteiga in low heat, pour the eggs and espere 20 segundos without mexer.
  3. Arraste with a espátula of the center para the bordas, repetindo until become creamy and still úmido.
  4. Serve imediatamente with the avocado for cima, the seeds and the scallions.
COOKING TIP: remove of the heat quando still parecer raw demais: the calor of the pan termina the cozimento.
↺ SMART SWAP: without avocado? Use 2 tbsp of heavy cream azedo with limão.

Protein Yogurt with Nut Granola

Preparation 5 minTotal 5 minYield 1Difficulty easy
320kcal / serving10 gcarbohydrates22 gprotein22 gfat

Ingredients

  • 170 g of yogurt natural integral
  • 1/3 cups of mix of nuts chopped (pará, caju, amêndoa)
  • 1 tbsp of coconut flakes unsweetened
  • 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1 tsp of sweetener or mel (opcional)
  • 5 strawberries chopped

Method

  1. Toast the nuts with the coconut flakes in frigideira seca for 2 minutes, mexendo sempre.
  2. Assemble the yogurt in a tigela, cover with the strawberries.
  3. Spread the granola morna for cima and polvilhe the cinnamon.
  4. Adoce só se necessário — the cinnamon já engana the paladar.
COOKING TIP: Nunca toast the granola perto of the telefone: castanha queima in 30 segundos and amarga tudo.
↺ SMART SWAP: Granola in lote: triplique the recipe and guarde 15 days in container fechado.

Simple Cauliflower Rice

Preparation 10 minTotal 15 minYield 4Difficulty easy
65kcal / serving5 gcarbohydrates4 gprotein3 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 cauliflower média in buquês
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic chopped
  • 1/4 of onion chopped
  • salt and fresh herbs

Method

  1. Process the cauliflower raw in pulsos curtos until become grãos of the tamanho of arroz (or grate in the ralador grosso).
  2. Sauté the garlic and the onion in the olive oil for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the cauliflower and sauté in high heat for 5-6 minutes, mexendo.
  4. Season with salt and finish with fresh herbs — não tampe, senão becomes purê.
COOKING TIP: Nunca process the cauliflower cooked: ela releases water and becomes papa. Sempre raw and in pulsos curtos.
↺ SMART SWAP: Base neutra: guarda 4 days in the refrigerator and works with any sobra of proteína for cima.

Zucchini Noodles with Homemade Tomato Sauce

Preparation 12 minTotal 30 minYield 2Difficulty easy
265kcal / serving14 gcarbohydrates12 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 abobrinhas grandes
  • 6 tomates maduros (or 1 lata of peeled whole tomatoes)
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil
  • basil, salt and pepper
  • parmesão grated to finish

Method

  1. make the sauce: sauté onion and garlic, add the tomato chopped and cook 20 minutes in low heat until encorpar.
  2. Cut the zucchini cut into strips finas with descascador or espiralizador.
  3. Sauté the zucchini for 90 segundos in the olive oil hot — só para amornar, nunca cozinhar.
  4. Add the sauce, mexa rapidamente and finish with basil and parmesão.
COOKING TIP: Salt the zucchini já espiralizada, let 10 minutes in the colander and seque with papel: corta the water que afina the sauce.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the peeled whole tomatoes for sauce of cauliflower batida with parmesão para a version still more baixa in carbo.

Garlic and Oil Palm Heart Noodles

Preparation 10 minTotal 20 minYield 2Difficulty easy
210kcal / serving7 gcarbohydrates8 gprotein17 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 vidro of palm hearts in fios (or espaguete of konjac)
  • 4 cloves of garlic laminados
  • 3 tbsp of olive oil
  • 1/4 of cup of parmesão grated
  • parsley, black pepper and salt

Method

  1. drain and seque bem the hearts of palm with papel-toalha.
  2. Heat the olive oil in low heat and brown the garlic laminado without deixar queimar.
  3. Add the hearts of palm and sauté 3 minutes in medium heat-alto para evaporar the water.
  4. turn off, mix the parmesão, the parsley and the pepper and serve imediatamente.
COOKING TIP: hearts of palm and konjac release a lot of water: dry bem antes of the sauté is the que impede the prato of become aguado.
↺ SMART SWAP: Add 1 cup of shrimp salteado para become prato principal completo.

Complete Chicken Salad with Yogurt Dressing

Preparation 15 minTotal 25 minYield 2Difficulty easy
340kcal / serving12 gcarbohydrates32 gprotein18 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast grelhado cut into strips
  • 4 cups of folhas variadas
  • 1/2 cucumber sliced
  • 1 tomato in gomos
  • 1/4 of avocado
  • 170 g of yogurt natural
  • 1 tsp of mustard, juice of 1/2 limão, olive oil, salt and ervas

Method

  1. Season the frango with salt, pepper and paprika and grill 5 minutes of cada lado.
  2. Assemble the folhas, the cucumber, the tomato and the avocado in two bowls.
  3. Mix the yogurt with the mustard, the limão, 1 tbsp of olive oil, salt and ervas.
  4. Cover with the frango warm and drizzle with the sauce in the hora of comer.
COOKING TIP: Salada with sauce separado lasts 2 days in the lunch container; with sauce junto, murcha in 3 horas.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the frango for tuna or chickpeas cooked (aumente the serving and some the carbos).

Eggplant Lasagna Bolognese

Preparation 30 minTotal 75 minYield 6Difficulty easy
355kcal / serving12 gcarbohydrates30 gprotein22 gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 berinjelas grandes sliced finas
  • 500 g of ground beef
  • 1 lata of peeled whole tomatoes
  • 1 onion and 3 cloves of garlic
  • 200 g of mozzarella fatiada
  • 100 g of parmesão grated
  • olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Salt the fatias of eggplant, let 20 minutes, enxágue and seque bem.

  4. Assemble in camadas — sauce, eggplant, mozzarella — terminando with sauce and parmesão. bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes.
COOKING TIP: dry the eggplant is obrigatório: without isso, the lasanha releases water and becomes sopa in the baking dish.
↺ SMART SWAP: swap the eggplant for fatias finas of zucchini, grelhando for menos tempo.

Low-Carb Kale Soup (No Potato)

Preparation 15 minTotal 35 minYield 4Difficulty easy
195kcal / serving9 gcarbohydrates14 gprotein12 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 cauliflower small in buquês
  • 200 g of sausage portuguesa or calabresa sliced
  • 1 maço of collard greens cut into strips finas
  • 1 onion and 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 L of chicken broth or water
  • 2 tbsp of olive oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Fry the sausage in pan grande until brown and set aside parte of the gordura.
  2. Sauté the onion and the garlic nessa gordura for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the cauliflower and the caldo, cook 15 minutes and beat in the liquidificador until become creamy.
  4. Return to the pan, add the kale cut into strips and the sausage and cook 5 minutes, without deixar the kale amolecer demais.
COOKING TIP: A kale entra in the final and kitchen só 5 minutes — ela deve become verde-viva, não marrom.
↺ SMART SWAP: version without embutido: swap the sausage for shredded chicken and add smoked paprika.

Ingredients

  • 500 g of pumpkin cabotiá diced
  • 200 ml of coconut milk unsweetened
  • 1 pedaço of 3 cm of ginger grated

  • 700 ml of vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp of olive oil, salt, pepper and scallions

↺ SMART SWAP: swap the ginger for curry powder and finish with cilantro para a perfil thai.

  4. turn off the oven and let the cheesecake esfriar lá dentro for 1 hora. cover with the frutas aquecidas with 1 tbsp of sweetener.
COOKING TIP: Esfriar dentro of the oven off evita the rachadura in the center — choque térmico is the vilão.
↺ SMART SWAP: without forma of aro? Use forma comum forrada with papel-manteiga cruzado para puxar the cheesecake.

Chocolate Truffles with Coconut Oil

Preparation 10 minTotal 40 minYield 12Difficulty easy
110kcal / serving4 gcarbohydrates2 gprotein10 gfat

Ingredients

  • 100 g of chocolate 85% chopped
  • 3 tbsp of oil of coco
  • 2 tbsp of heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp of eritritol powder
  • 2 tbsp of unsweetened cocoa powder para enrolar
  • 1 pitada of salt and gotas of baunilha

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate with the oil of coco in banho-maria or in the micro-ondas in pulsos of 20 segundos.

  3. Refrigerate for 30 minutes, until set the suficiente para modelar.
  4. Shape bolinhas with the mãos and coat in the unsweetened cocoa powder.
COOKING TIP: Melt the chocolate in pulsos curtos, mexendo the cada 20 segundos: in heat direto ele queima and is arenoso.

Low-Carb Peanut Brittle (Paçoca Style)

Preparation 8 minTotal 20 minYield 12Difficulty easy
115kcal / serving4 gcarbohydrates5 gprotein10 gfat

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups of peanuts torrado skinless and without salt
  • 2 tbsp of almond flour
  • 2 tbsp of eritritol powder
  • 1 pitada of salt
  • 1 tsp of oil of coco (opcional)

Method

  1. Process the peanuts in pulsos until become a farofa úmida — cuidado para não become pasta.
  2. Add the almond flour, the eritritol and the salt and pulse more 3 vezes.
  3. Se estiver very seco, add the oil of coco and mix.
  4. Press the mistura in forminhas or in a container forrado, cut in quadrados and let set for 15 minutes.
COOKING TIP: Process in pulsos curtos and pare antes: peanuts processado demais becomes manteiga and the paçoca não firma.
↺ SMART SWAP: Guarde in container fechado for until 10 days in local fresh.
